# Spanish / French bridge

1847 of the owner's 2427 roots, ordered by how often he uses them. Counts are from `DevComms/vocabulary_dictionary.txt`.

**Every entry is unverified** — written by hand from Claude's knowledge, not from a citable dictionary.

The **bridge** column holds a Latin-layer English word meaning roughly the same thing, for words whose own form does not carry across. Empty means the word travels as itself.

- 1573 travel as themselves; 274 need a substitute word first.
- 100 have no cognate and must be memorised.
- 6 are false friends.

## False friends

| word | uses | español | français | the trap |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| actual | 117 | real / verdadero | réel / véritable | *actual* / *actuel* mean **current, present-day** — not *real* |
| current | 64 | actual | actuel | the Romance word for this is *actual* / *actuel* — the mirror of the trap above |
| assume | 38 | asumir / suponer | assumer / supposer | *asumir* / *assumer* lean toward **take on, shoulder**; for 'assume that' use *suponer* / *supposer* |
| large | 13 | grande / largo | large / grand | *largo* in Spanish means **long**, not large; French *large* means **wide** |
| eventually | 11 | finalmente | finalement | *eventualmente* / *éventuellement* mean **possibly**, not *in the end* |
| support | 10 | apoyar / soportar | soutenir / supporter | *soportar* / *supporter* lean toward **endure, put up with**; for 'support a project' use *apoyar* / *soutenir* |
