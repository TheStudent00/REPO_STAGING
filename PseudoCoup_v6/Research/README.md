# Research

Flexible, but contained: every line of inquiry gets its own nested
subfolder — no loose files at this level.

Carried from PCv5's research file system (its genuinely good
lesson):

- One experiment, one directory, self-contained and runnable alone.
- Every experiment states its falsifiable acceptance criterion
  before it runs; the run's outputs are the record.
- Measure, don't estimate; numbers come from commands actually run.
- Superseded experiments move to the local `.archive/`.

Research findings feed course-corrections in Planning and
candidates for Tools.
