REJECTED (canon9's own attempt, DISPROVED by the ground-truth gate --

    and %r10,%rax                           %rax = %rax & %r10
    shl $7,%rax                             %rax = shift_left(%rax, $7)
    mov $0,%r10b                            %r10b = $0
    movzx %r10b,%r10                        %r10 = zero_extend(%r10b)

    and $-1,%r11d                           %r11d = %r11d & $-1

    ROOT CAUSE: `%r11`/`%r9` are zero-extended 32-bit fields (the

    always-non-negative pair, which is a DIFFERENT relation than the
    32-bit signed compare the source `a > b` means whenever a or b's
    32-bit top bit is a genuine sign bit.

FIX (b) worked example -- c/op_427 (`(int64_t)(int32_t)a & b`)

BEFORE (canon4/5/7's own "good" text -- ALSO wrong, a pre-existing
canon4 erasure defect documented independently by canon8_behaviour_
check.py's own header before this lap started):
    unit: c/op_427   source: canon10_text (kept, itself defective)
    movslq %edi,%r10   %r10 = sign_extend(%edi)
    mov %r10d,%eax      %eax = %r10d
    and %rsi,%rax        %rax = %rax & %rsi
    ret                    return %rax

    `mov %r10d,%eax` is a 32-BIT WRITE -- it auto-zero-extends %rax's
    upper 32 bits, silently DESTROYING the sign-extension `movslq`
    just performed. Real ship code (canon4's own `mnem`): `movslq
    %edi,%rax; and %rsi,%rax; ret` -- no intermediate 32-bit copy at
    all.

REJECTED (canon9's own attempt -- correct-looking but DISPROVED,
60+ instructions bit-serially reconstructing the sign bit 33 times):
    unit: c/op_427   source: job2_candidate_text (rejected, abridged)
    mov %rsi,%rax; not %rax; mov %rdi,%r10; shr $31,%r10; and $1,%r10b;
    not %r10b; shl $1,%r10b; ... [30 more shr/and/not/shl/or steps,
    one per sign-bit copy] ...; shl $32,%r10; mov %rdi,%r11;
    and $-1,%r11d; not %r11d; or %r11,%r10; or %r10,%rax; not %rax; ret

    ROOT CAUSE: z3's own simplify() normal form does not only push
    NOT through AND/OR (De Morgan, already recognized) -- it ALSO
    pushes NOT through CONCAT (`~Concat(A,B) == Concat(~A,~B)`, bit-
    exact, concatenation only places bits). The sign-extension of `a`
    (VEX's own idiom: 33 copies of bit 31, concatenated with `a`
    itself) sat inside a De Morgan'd OR operand that z3 had already
    NOT-distributed through its Concat, so the renderer's De Morgan
    recognizer (which only checked for a bare `bvnot`-wrapped operand)
    missed it, fell through to the raw bit-serial reconstruction, and
    never gave the concat's own "this is a sign-extend" recognizer a
    chance to see the pattern.

AFTER (canon11 -- FIX (b): `_negate_of()` also recognizes a concat of
all-bvnot parts as a negation, so the sign-extend concat is un-
negated, the existing De Morgan fold and sign-extend-concat
recognizer both fire, and the 33-part reconstruction collapses to one
`movsxd`):
    unit: c/op_427   source: canon11_text (STAGE 1, PROVED_EQUAL)
    mov %rsi,%rax        %rax = %rsi
    mov %rdi,%r10          %r10 = %rdi
    and $-1,%r10d             %r10d = %r10d & $-1
    movsxd %r10d,%r10           %r10 = sign_extend(%r10d)
    and %r10,%rax                 %rax = %rax & %r10
    ret                              return %rax

    z3 proves this text equal to the unit's own real ship code for
    every register value -- PROVED_EQUAL, converged. Not byte-
    identical to canon4's `mnem` (5 lines vs. 3: the extract-then-
    sign-extend still goes through the generic `extract`+`movsxd`
    path rather than fusing into one `movslq`) -- CORRECT, proven,
    but not maximally terse; fusing that pair into a single `movslq`
    is a legitimate follow-on, left honestly unfinished rather than
    claimed done.
